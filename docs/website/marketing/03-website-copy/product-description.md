## Long (~400 words) - Full product page, press release, detailed partner brief

WB Listora is a WordPress directory plugin that gives site operators a fully-functioning public directory in Free and a complete monetization stack in Pro - both on the same codebase, both released together.

**The Free plugin** ships 11 native Gutenberg blocks: listing grid, card, search, map, detail, reviews, submission, categories, featured carousel, calendar, and user dashboard. The search engine runs on a dedicated denormalized index table - not `WP_Query` on post content - supporting full-text, geo radius, and faceted filtering with "Search this area" drag-to-update bounds. The 6-step setup wizard auto-creates pages, loads demo content across 9 verticals (128+ seeded listings), and has a working directory ready in under 30 minutes. Anti-spam runs 6 layers deep: honeypot, per-IP rate limits, reCAPTCHA v3 or Cloudflare Turnstile, Akismet, keyword blacklist, and URL density cap - all active by default. Business claims, reviews with helpful votes, frontend submission with conditional fields and draft saving, and 15 email notification templates are all included in Free.

**WB Listora Pro** extends Free with the business model infrastructure: a credit-based pricing system with Hold-and-Commit activation (credits held at plan selection, committed when the listing activates - no partial charges on failures), 7 payment paths through WooCommerce, WooCommerce Subscriptions, MemberPress, Paid Memberships Pro, WooMemberships, Stripe direct, and PayPal direct. Pro adds lead forms that replace the contact form with analytics and CRM-ready delivery, multi-criteria reviews with photo attachments, verification badges with configurable types and optional expiration, side-by-side listing comparison with a floating selection bar, a moderators team with individually grantable capability scopes, full audit logging, per-listing and directory-wide analytics, outgoing webhooks, white-label admin branding, BuddyPress activity stream integration, SEO auto-generated landing pages, saved searches with email alerts, and the Needs Marketplace - a reverse directory where buyers post requirements and businesses filter and respond with quotes.

The developer surface is extensive: 259 fired hooks (133 actions + 126 filters), 58 REST endpoints in Free plus 65 in Pro, 8 WP-CLI commands, WooCommerce-style template overrides, a single Interactivity API store, and Action Scheduler bundled in Free for reliable background processing. Pro adds 32 feature modules, all toggle-gated for clean isolation.

Requires WordPress 6.9+, PHP 7.4+, MySQL 5.7+ or MariaDB 10.3+. Translation-ready with RTL stylesheet support. Multisite and headless/REST-first architectures supported.
