# Short Ad Scripts - Paid Social & Video Ads

Three lengths, thirteen scripts total. Each is written with a first-frame hook that communicates without sound - critical for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube pre-roll where most viewers never unmute.

**Placement guide:**
- 15-second: Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts (skip-eligible pre-roll)
- 30-second: YouTube non-skippable, Twitter/X video, LinkedIn, Meta feed ads
- 60-second: Paid social longer-form, YouTube in-stream, LinkedIn video ads

**Writing rules applied across all scripts:**
- Hook works on-screen without voiceover (text overlay visible in frame 1)
- Problem in the first 3 seconds
- Solution named by second 8
- One specific feature - not a feature list
- CTA is a single URL or action word
- No em-dashes. No banned words.

## Production Notes (all ad lengths)

**Silent viewing:** Assume no audio for the first 3 seconds of every ad. The hook text overlay must communicate the problem or benefit without sound. Every 15s and 30s ad should be watchable end-to-end with captions only.

**Captions:** Burned in for all social placements. White text, 2px black outline, sans-serif, center-bottom position.

**Aspect ratios per placement:**
| Placement | Ratio |
|---|---|
| Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts | 9:16 |
| Twitter/X video, LinkedIn feed | 1:1 |
| YouTube in-stream, landing page | 16:9 |

**Brand consistency:** All ads use ocean blue (#0077B6 reference) + coral accent (#FF6B6B reference). End card is always: WB Listora logo + wbcomdesigns.com + placement-specific CTA text. 3-second hold on end card minimum.

**Music:** Match the script mood. See [Shot List Template](shot-list-template.md) for full music guide and BPM ranges. Keep at 15-20% under voiceover. Full volume on end card (2-second swell).

**Targeting note:** 15s ads are upper-funnel awareness. 30s ads are consideration. 60s ads are bottom-of-funnel for retargeting audiences. Match the ad length to the audience temperature.
