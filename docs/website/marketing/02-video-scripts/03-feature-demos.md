# Video Scripts - Per-Feature 60-Second Clips (Canonical)

Thirteen focused short videos. Each stands alone for social, paid ads, or "feature deep-dive" thumbnails on your YouTube channel. Each is approximately 60 seconds, scripted, with reference screenshots from the image library and on-screen text overlay specs.

**Format for every script:**
- **Hook:** The first thing the viewer hears and sees - must work without sound
- **Body:** What the feature does and why it matters
- **CTA:** One action for the viewer to take
- **Length target:** 52-58 seconds after final edit (fits YouTube Shorts 60s ceiling)

---

## 1. Search & Filters (Free)

**On screen:** Visitor types "italian" into the search bar - autocomplete dropdown appears. Clicks Near Me - results re-sort with a distance badge on each card. Checks "Outdoor Seating" in the sidebar - results update instantly. URL bar updates to reflect the filter state.

**On-screen text overlays:**
- 0:00 - "What if visitors found what they need in 3 seconds?"
- 0:08 - "Live suggestions as you type"
- 0:18 - "Near Me geo-sort"
- 0:28 - "Faceted filters - no page reload"
- 0:40 - "Shareable filtered URLs"

**Hook:** "What good is a directory if visitors can't find what they need in 3 seconds?"

**Body:**
> WB Listora's search runs on a denormalized index - not the slow `WP_Query` most directory plugins rely on. Type a keyword, see suggestions immediately. Click Near Me and results re-sort by distance using Haversine geolocation. Tick a facet in the sidebar - category, location, amenity - and results update without a page reload. The URL updates too, so visitors can share or bookmark exactly what they searched. Scales to 100,000 listings without slowing down.

**CTA:** "Try the live demo at wblistora.com."

**Music suggestion:** Upbeat, crisp. BPM 130-140. Epidemic Sound "Upbeat Corporate" category.

## Production Notes (all 13 clips)

**Pace:** 60 seconds is tight. Trim the body if needed - keep the hook and CTA pristine. Target 52-58 seconds in final edit.

**Captions:** Burn in for silent autoplay. Most social views are sound-off. White text, 2px black outline.

**On-screen text overlays:** Use the timestamps in each script as placement guides. Font: Inter Bold or similar sans. Size: 26px at 1920x1080. Color: white text on a semi-transparent ocean-blue bar at the bottom third.

**Format:** Master 16:9 for YouTube and the docs site. Re-cut 9:16 for Reels and Shorts - crop to the center action area. Re-cut 1:1 for LinkedIn feed.

**End card:** Consistent across all 13 clips. WB Listora logo + wblistora.com + "Free / Pro" label. 5-second hold.

**Branding:** Ocean blue primary (#0077B6 reference) with coral accent (#FF6B6B reference) for overlay bars.
