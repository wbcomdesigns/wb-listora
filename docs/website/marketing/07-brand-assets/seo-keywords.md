## Notes on usage

**Volume estimates** are category-level benchmarks. Run the full keyword list through SpyFu or Ahrefs before committing to a content calendar. Prioritise by: (commercial intent x monthly volume) / difficulty.

**Quick wins:** Keywords in Group 5 (feature-specific) and comparison terms for WPBDP and ListingPro have low difficulty and high commercial intent. These are the fastest pages to rank.

**Compounding opportunity:** Migration guides ("how to migrate from Directorist") capture both informational and high-intent visitors at the moment of maximum frustration with a competitor.

**Brand terms** (WB Listora, WB Listora Pro, WB Listora pricing) should be owned pages with schema markup. They have low difficulty because competition is nil - only WB Listora ranks for its own name.
