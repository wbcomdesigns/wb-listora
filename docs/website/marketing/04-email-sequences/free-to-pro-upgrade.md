## Email 3 - Week 4: The trust and scale angle

**Subject:** How directories earn repeat visitors

**Preview text:** Reviews matter. Verification matters more.

---

Hi {{first_name}},

Visitors come back to directories they trust. Two things build that trust faster than anything else: great reviews and verified listings.

**On reviews:**

Free includes a solid review system - star ratings, written reviews, helpful votes, owner replies, moderation queue. That's enough to start.

But as your directory grows, a single overall star rating stops being informative. Visitors want to know: was the food good even if the service was slow? Was the hotel location great but the cleanliness disappointing?

Pro's multi-criteria reviews let reviewers rate individual aspects separately - Food, Service, Value, Ambiance for a restaurant, or any set of criteria you define per listing type. Each gets its own score. Photo reviews let reviewers attach images. The composite breakdown displays on the listing detail page and in search facets.

**On verification:**

Verification badges (Pro) let you mark vetted listings as Verified Owner, Top Rated, or whatever badge taxonomy fits your directory. Badges appear on listing cards, detail pages, and in search results - they're visible at every touchpoint. You control who gets them and when they expire.

Visitors click verified listings more. Vendors want to be verified. That dynamic creates the feedback loop a healthy directory needs.
