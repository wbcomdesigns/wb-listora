The structural gap

There is no way to create a listing from the app. Every listing must originate on the web, so the app can never grow the directory it displays. Three documented member journeys — submit, guest-submit with email verify, and edit — have no app path at all.

The missing artefact

The app has a thorough gap audit, but it covers contract bugs — map tiles, currency format, nonces. There is no docs/FEATURE-COVERAGE.md: the parity matrix that would have made these gaps visible as a release gate. Its absence is why the app can feel finished while submission and account deletion are absent.

Method & confidence

Catalogue
Plugin-owned CAPABILITIES.md in both repos — 113 Free rows, 167 Pro capabilities. Not re-enumerated from the app side.
Routes
Live GET /wp-json/listora/v1. The plugin’s own catalogue warns its manifest is unreliable for routes, so the index was probed directly.
App surface
Every endpoint referenced in api/, hooks/ and app/, plus each tab screen read by hand.
Confidence
Endpoint- and screen-level. This establishes what is reachable, not what is bug-free — no screen was runtime-tested for this report.
